# 🏫 Digital Welfare Check Form

A custom-built, offline-first morning check-in application designed to support student welfare in primary schools.

## 🛑 The Problems It Solves
* **Morning Fatigue:** Flags pupils walking very long distances to school.
* **Nutrition Gaps:** Alerts teachers to students who missed breakfast or lack school lunch.
* **Hidden Vulnerabilities:** Gives shy or quiet children a private voice to report sickness or a lack of home study lights.

## 💡 Key Features
* **Tkinter UI:** A large, easy-to-read morning form suitable for young pupils (P.1 to P.7).
* **Turtle Graphics Radar:** A real-time, color-coded dashboard that flashes **Bright Red** if a child requires immediate teacher attention.
* **Role-Based Security:** Class teachers are locked to their own rooms, while the Head Teacher master password (`headteacher777`) unlocks a comprehensive school-wide ledger with pre-loaded demo data.
* **100% Offline Ledger:** Saves all logs continuously into a light text file, making it highly affordable and practical for schools with limited internet or power stability.
